## 2. FACTS (from the two slides as shown)

- Issuing body: 中小及新創企業署 (SME & Startup Administration).
- Stated purpose: implement Executive Yuan's "中小微企業多元振興發展計畫" and MOEA's "AI新十大建設" — drive domestic SMEs toward cross-domain (跨域) co-created technology/service value, using tech or business-model innovation, to accelerate technology development, commercialization, and new market opportunity.
- Three proposal tracks: (1) Agentic AI 與領域應用, (2) Sustainable AI 與災防管理, (3) Physical AI 技術與服務.
- R&D outcomes must reach a three-stage commercialization goal: 場域驗證 (field validation) → 國內落地 (domestic deployment) → 國際出海 (international expansion).
- Eligibility: at least 2 (or more) SMEs must apply jointly; the lead applicant (主導廠商) must be an SME.
- Bonus-scoring items (可酌予加分): (1) at least one alliance member is a startup; (2) a committed, verifiable carbon-reduction figure at project close; (3) extending the R&D result into international/third-country markets.
- Funding: subsidy capped at NT$9,000,000 per application; subsidy cannot exceed 50% of total project expenditure; stated relationship is 補助款 ≤ 自籌款 ≤ 實收資本額 (subsidy ≤ self-funded portion ≤ paid-in capital).
- Execution window: from the 1st of the month following approval through 116/10/31 (2027-10-31 ROC calendar), estimated ~9 months.
- Expected outcome at project close: 已試量產、試營運或上市銷售/服務 (trial production, trial operation, or market sales/service already reached); 若獲海外營收尤佳 (overseas revenue is explicitly preferred, not required).
- Agentic AI track's listed sub-technology areas: agentic frameworks (autonomous task decomposition/planning/execution/tool use), domain knowledge base + RAG, tool/API interoperability (explicitly names MCP/A2A), multimodal perception, "trustworthiness and evaluation" (可信任與評測 — hallucination suppression, traceable/auditable decision logs, human hand-back on abnormal cases), model tuning/lightweight models, cloud/edge deployment.
- Listed proposal shapes: (1) AI-optimize an existing process; (2) existing product + AI innovation — 1.0→2.0 value-add/subscription, or product-line upgrade, or business-model/scenario migration.
- Slide 2 lists 6 named prior cross-domain-alliance case studies (113/114/115 program years) as precedent examples of the alliance format — not evidence about this specific October cycle's difficulty or acceptance rate.
- **From the 2026-09-16 updated Agentic AI slide:** the track explicitly covers discriminative, generative, and agentic AI, and states "不以具備完整代理能力為申請門檻" — possessing complete agentic capability is NOT an application threshold. This lowers the bar for what counts as qualifying technology; a CRC/Commercial-Assurance-style system does not need to be a fully autonomous agent to fit the track.
- The updated slide adds a 12-item numbered example-scenario list across 一級/二級/三級產業 (primary/manufacturing/services industries), explicitly stated as illustrative, not exhaustive ("不以此為限；符合本主軸範疇之其他情境均得提案"). **Scenario #12 is "專業服務文件審閱與法遵作業"** (professional-services document review and regulatory/compliance operations) — the closest direct-language match yet to SI8's own Commercial Assurance use case.
- **New, 2026-09-16 — non-PRC supply-chain requirement (slide titled 非紅供應鏈說明：共同規範與依研發標的分流):** not present in the Sept 15 slides; see full breakdown in §7 below.

## 7. Non-PRC Supply-Chain Requirement (new, surfaced 2026-09-16) — engineering audit needed

**FLAG: this is an engineering compliance question, not just a BD data point.** Before SI8 represents itself as eligible or compliant, its actual dependency/supply chain needs auditing against this requirement — do not assume compliance.

**What the slide states (草案/draft; verify against the final RFP):**

- General rule: software, hardware, firmware, embedded systems, and cloud services used for the funded R&D must not be mainland-China (incl. Hong Kong/Macau) branded or manufactured; a system/platform modified from a mainland-China-origin base is still treated as a mainland-China product.
- **For AI software/services specifically — mandatory exclusion:** AI models, APIs, and services originating from mainland China cannot be used. This explicitly extends to **open-source, open-weight, fine-tuned, and distilled models** built on a mainland-China-origin base model.
- **Disclosure requirement** for non-AI open-source packages: name, version, source, and SBOM (software bill of materials).
- **Encouraged (not mandatory):** Taiwan-domestic models, AI services, cloud platforms, and compute resources.
- **Verification stages:** application, review, execution, and closeout. During execution, applicants may need to supply procurement evidence, supplier declarations, or SBOMs on request. At closeout, a final BOM is required.
- **Enforcement:** false information, refusal to cooperate with verification, or use of prohibited components can result in expense disallowance and subsidy clawback.

**Audit checklist (not yet performed):**
- Foundation/LLM model providers and specific models in use (incl. any accessed via API aggregators, where the underlying model origin may not be obvious).
- Embedding models and rerankers.
- Any open-source, open-weight, fine-tuned, or distilled model in the stack, and its base-model lineage.
- Non-AI open-source software dependencies (name/version/source, and whether SBOM generation is currently possible at all).
- Cloud/compute providers.
- Any component with mainland-China origin, ownership, or manufacture, direct or indirect.

**Decision discipline:** do not assume SI8 already complies. Do not change strategically important architecture solely to satisfy this grant without weighing the tradeoff on its own merits. If SI8 already complies, or can comply via low-cost substitutions that don't harm product strategy, that becomes a genuine application-strengthening fact — not before it's actually checked.
