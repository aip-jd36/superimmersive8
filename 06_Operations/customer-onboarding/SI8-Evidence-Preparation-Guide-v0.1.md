## What This Guide Is For

When you commission an SI8 Campaign Clearance Assessment, you are engaging an independent reviewer to evaluate the evidence behind your AI-generated content. This guide helps you understand what that reviewer will look for and how to prepare your evidence package before submitting.

You do not need a perfect evidence package to receive a useful assessment. Part of what SI8 documents is what evidence exists, what is missing, and how those gaps affect commercial confidence. Incomplete submissions are common, and the assessment is designed to handle them.

---

## What SI8 Assesses

SI8 evaluates evidence across seven areas of commercial risk:

- **Who created the content** and on whose authority it was submitted
- **Whether the AI tools used permit commercial deployment** — subscription tier, licensing terms, output rights
- **The nature and extent of human creative involvement** in the final output
- **Whether the content contains third-party copyrighted material** — characters, artwork, architectural works, design elements
- **Whether any real person's likeness, face, or voice appears** in the content, and whether consent or rights are documented
- **Whether the production process is documented** — workflow, tools, generation steps
- **Whether the evidence submitted is internally consistent** — dates, versions, and records align

SI8 does not provide legal advice or legal opinions. The assessment produces a commercial confidence evaluation based on the evidence available at time of submission.

---

## Evidence and Commercial Confidence

The more complete your evidence package, the higher the commercial confidence SI8 can express in the assessment. A well-documented submission — commercial licenses, workflow description, audio rights — typically supports a clear outcome.

When evidence is missing, SI8 documents the gap, assesses its commercial impact, and reflects that in the outcome and the report. A missing item does not automatically produce a negative outcome. It produces a specific finding: *what is missing and what that means for commercial deployment*.

> Do not worry if you do not have every item listed below. Bring what you have. The assessment is designed to work with incomplete evidence packages.

## What to Prepare

Gather the following before you submit. Not all items apply to every project — use the Evidence Checklist at the end of this guide to identify what is relevant to yours.

### 1. Your Final Content

The video file or a link to the final deliverable in the highest quality available. If the content is a campaign with multiple assets, include all versions intended for commercial use.

### 2. AI Tools and Platforms

The name of every AI tool used in production — for video generation, audio synthesis, voice, upscaling, or editing. Include version or generation model if known (e.g., Runway Gen-3 Alpha, Kling 1.6). If you used multiple tools in sequence, describe the order.

### 3. Commercial License Documentation

Confirmation that each AI tool was used on a commercially licensed plan at the time of generation. This is typically a subscription receipt, invoice, or account screenshot showing an active paid plan. This is the most commonly missing item in submissions — gather it now.

### 4. Human Creative Contribution

A brief description of how the content was directed and refined. This does not need to be a formal document — a paragraph describing the workflow is sufficient. What was prompted, what was edited, what creative decisions were made by a human, and who made them.

### 5. Audio and Music Rights

The source of all audio in the content — AI-generated, licensed music, original recording, or stock. If music is licensed, include the license or purchase receipt. If audio was AI-generated, include the tool name and commercial license confirmation.

### 6. Third-Party and Stock Assets

If any stock footage, photography, graphics, or other licensed assets were incorporated into the final output, include the license documentation.

### 7. Likeness and Performer Documentation

If the content features a real person's face, voice, or distinctive persona, include a signed talent release or consent documentation. If no real person appears in the content, note that in your submission — it helps the reviewer confirm the assessment more quickly.

## If Evidence Is Missing

If you do not have receipts for your AI tools, do not remember the exact version used, or did not preserve generation logs — submit what you have and describe what is missing in your workflow notes. SI8 will document the gap, assess its commercial significance, and include that analysis in your report.

A gap in documentation is not the same as a problem with the content. Many gaps are addressable: if the reviewer identifies missing evidence that you could provide, the report will note that.

## After You Submit

Once your submission is complete and payment is confirmed, SI8 will begin the review. You will receive your Campaign Clearance Assessment Report within five business days. The report will include:

- A summary of what was assessed
- Findings across the evidence areas reviewed
- An overall commercial confidence assessment
- Residual risks and recommended next steps
- SI8's standard limitation of assurance statement
