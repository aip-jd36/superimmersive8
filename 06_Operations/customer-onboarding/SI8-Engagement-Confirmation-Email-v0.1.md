# SI8 Engagement Confirmation Email
**Version:** 0.1 · July 2026
**Status:** ACTIVE — send manually after a customer purchases or confirms an SI8 Campaign Clearance Package
**Audience:** Internal template — do not share directly; adapt and send from JD's email

---

## Notes for Sender

This email is designed to be sent manually by JD for the first several assessments. It should feel like a personal email from a professional firm, not an automated notification. Adapt the opening and tone to the specific relationship — if you've already exchanged several LinkedIn messages, open with a personal reference.

Send within 24 hours of payment confirmation.

Attach (or link) the SI8 Evidence Preparation Guide.

---

## Subject Lines (choose one based on context)

- `Your SI8 Campaign Clearance Assessment — next steps`
- `SI8 Assessment confirmed — here's how to prepare`
- `Re: [Campaign/Project Name] — your SI8 assessment is underway`

## Email Body

---

Hi [First name],

Thank you for commissioning an SI8 Campaign Clearance Assessment — I'm glad we could make this work.

**What you've purchased:** A full SI8 Certified review of [campaign/project description — e.g., "your AI-generated social campaign for [client/brand]"]. This includes an independent evaluation of the commercial rights and evidence behind the content, a structured findings report, and an overall commercial confidence assessment.

**What you'll receive:** Your Campaign Clearance Assessment Report, delivered within five business days of your completed submission. The report will walk through the evidence reviewed, findings across each area of commercial risk, an overall assessment, and any residual risks worth noting.

**What to do next:** I've attached the SI8 Evidence Preparation Guide — it describes what evidence to gather before you submit and how to think about the submission process. The short version: bring what you have. If evidence is incomplete, we document that as part of the assessment rather than treating it as a barrier.

[If using the CertForm portal:]
Once you have your evidence together, you can submit through the secure portal at **app.superimmersive8.com/certify**. Your payment is already confirmed — just work through the submission form and upload what you have.

[If handling submission manually for first assessments:]
Once you have your evidence together, reply to this email and we'll coordinate submission directly. I'll send you a simple intake form to complete.

If you have any questions before you submit — about what to include, what to do if something is missing, or anything else — just reply here. I'm the reviewer on this assessment and I'd rather you ask now than wonder later.

Looking forward to reviewing your work.

JD Chang
SuperImmersive 8 · PMF Strategy Inc.
jd@superimmersive8.com
superimmersive8.com

*Please note: SI8's Campaign Clearance Assessment evaluates evidence and commercial risk. It does not constitute legal advice, a legal opinion, or a guarantee of commercial safety. The full limitation of assurance statement appears in your Assessment Report.*
