## Section 2 — Evidence Checklist

**Purpose:** Inventory what evidence was provided for each control domain. Record your judgment and notes. Do not make findings yet — this is evidence inventory only.

**Time target:** 25 minutes

**Judgment options for each control:**
- **Verified** — adequate evidence provided and reviewed; control is satisfied
- **Partially Verified** — some evidence provided but incomplete; note what's missing
- **Not Provided** — no evidence provided for this control; log in Section 3
- **Not Applicable** — this control does not apply to this submission (state why)

## Section 3 — Evidence Gap Log

**Purpose:** For each control marked "Not Provided" or "Partially Verified" in Section 2, record: what's missing, whether it is addressable by the customer, and the commercial impact of the gap.

**Time target:** 10 minutes

*If no gaps, write "No material evidence gaps identified" and proceed to Section 4.*

---

**Gap format:**

```
Gap ID:               [G01, G02, ...]
Related control:      [e.g., R02 — AI Tool Commercial License]
What is missing:      [Specific description of the missing evidence]
Addressable?          [Yes — customer could provide this / No — evidence cannot be retroactively created / Unknown]
Commercial impact:    [Low / Medium / High]
Impact description:   [What this gap means for commercial confidence — e.g., "Without commercial license
                       confirmation for Kling, SI8 cannot verify the output rights for one of the primary
                       generation tools. This affects the Output Rights assessment (R04) and the overall
                       commercial confidence."]
```

## Section 4 — Findings Log

**Purpose:** Record specific, evidence-based observations about commercial risk by domain. A finding is what you concluded from the evidence, not just what was provided. Findings may be positive (no issue in this domain) or negative (specific risk identified).

**Time target:** 20 minutes

**Finding format:**

```
Finding ID:           [F01, F02, ...]
Domain:               [e.g., Commercial Rights & Licensing]
Finding:              [Clear statement of what was established or what risk was identified]
Evidence basis:       [What specific evidence supports this finding]
Commercial impact:    [Low / Medium / High]
Addressable?          [Yes / No / Conditional]
```

*Write a finding for each domain where you have a substantive conclusion — positive or negative. You do not need a finding for every control. Aim for 3–8 findings per assessment.*

**Time target:** 10 minutes

```

[2–4 sentences explaining the key factors that drove this outcome.
```
