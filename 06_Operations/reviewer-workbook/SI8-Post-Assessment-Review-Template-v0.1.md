# SI8 Post-Assessment Review
**Version:** 0.1
**Effective date:** July 3, 2026
**Status:** ACTIVE — complete one copy after every assessment
**Audience:** Internal only. Do not share with customers. Do not attach to Assessment Report.
**Purpose:** Capture operational learning after every assessment. Primary input to Methodology v0.2 and Reviewer Workbook v0.2.

---

**Instructions:** Complete this review within 24 hours of delivering the Assessment Report, while the assessment is still fresh. Honest, specific notes are more valuable than polished ones. There is no minimum or maximum length — write what's useful.
