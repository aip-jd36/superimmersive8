- **Governed claim co-occurrence (authoring guideline, added 2026-08-24, following the CRC Governed Claim Relationship / Baseline-Exception diagnostic of the same date):** when a topic/row carries more than one CRC-eligible claim that may retrieve simultaneously for the same goal (Kling's `kling-commercial-use-baseline` + `kling-commercial-use-member` is the current live example), each claim's own `CRC Candidate Statement` must (1) remain independently accurate when rendered alone, (2) remain coherent when rendered adjacent to any other simultaneously-applicable sibling claim, and (3) never depend on incidental claims-array/fixture order for its substantive meaning — that order is a storage artifact, not a governed decision. `applicability_requirements` determines only whether a claim currently applies; it does not establish, and must never be read by anyone (author or Retrieval/BI) as establishing, that one claim is an exception to, overrides, qualifies, or supersedes another — nor does declaration order. A candidate statement may use self-contained conditional wording that is itself supported by its own governed proposition (e.g. "If [governed condition]...", as the Kling Member claim already does) — but authors must not add phrases such as "However," "As an exception," "Despite the above," or "This overrides" merely to make concatenation with an unspecified future sibling read more smoothly, unless the corresponding relationship is itself governed and evidenced. No such claim-relationship schema exists today; the diagnostic above found one not currently justified by evidence (a single live instance) and recommended documenting this authoring discipline instead. Purely an authoring convention — introduces no new runtime semantics, applicability behavior, ordering rule, or Bounded Interpretation logic.
