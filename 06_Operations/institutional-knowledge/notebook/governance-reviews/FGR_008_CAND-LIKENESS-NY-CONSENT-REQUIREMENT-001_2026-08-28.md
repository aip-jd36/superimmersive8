## 4. The recognizability issue

**4.1 — Does Candidate A actually require the recognizability construction?** No. Elements 1–3 (§ 3 above) form a complete, self-standing, useful governed rule on their own: a living person's statutorily-named attributes, used for advertising/trade in New York without written consent, creates civil (and, per § 50, potentially criminal) exposure. Nothing about that rule depends on how broadly "portrait, picture, likeness" is judicially construed — the recognizability sentence answers a *different* question (how far the statutory terms reach), not a precondition of the core rule's truth.

**4.2 — If removed, does a narrower useful proposition remain fully supported?** Yes — see § 3, elements 1–3, all Class A. Nothing is lost from the rule's practical usefulness to a CRC user; what is lost is only a specific interpretive gloss that was never more than secondary-sourced to begin with.

**4.3 — Does the current proposition overstate the evidence?** Yes, in presentation if not in substance. The package's own prose already discloses the secondary-only tier in its "Evidence tier" line and "Evidence limitations" section — but the proposition sentence itself, and the "CRC could safely say" wording, present the recognizability construction in the same declarative register as the fully-verified statutory sentences immediately before it ("New York courts have construed... New York courts have read..."), with no in-line qualifier. A reader of the proposition or the candidate CRC wording alone — without separately reading the Evidence-tier disclosure — would reasonably take it as equally well-established. This is a real presentation defect, not merely a stylistic one: it lets (B)-tier material read as (A)-tier material at the exact two places (the proposition and the CRC-facing wording) most likely to be quoted or relied on downstream.

**4.4 — Disposition of this issue:** **NARROW**, not defer-whole-candidate and not reject-whole-candidate. Remove the recognizability sentence from both the governed proposition and the candidate CRC statement; do not delete the underlying research — preserve it explicitly as a disclosed, not-yet-evidence-supported companion consideration for a possible future sub-candidate once *Loftus*'s full text is obtained (see § 17 exact wording, below). This is the smallest evidence-faithful correction available, consistent with this review's own governing instruction to prefer narrowing over fabricating case-law certainty or over withholding a fully-supported core rule merely because one attached sentence isn't ready.
