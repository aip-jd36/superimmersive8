## 1. Purpose

This document defines the intended experience for every SI8 customer who commissions a Campaign Clearance Assessment.

It is a vision document, not an operations manual. It describes what the customer should experience at every stage of their engagement with SI8 — what they should feel, what they should understand, and what SI8 should communicate. The Operational Journey document maps these intentions to the actual workflows and artifacts SI8 uses to deliver them.

The Customer Journey should remain relatively stable. Changes to it represent a change in SI8's service philosophy, not a process update. The Operational Journey evolves frequently; the Customer Journey should not.

---

## 2. Customer Experience Philosophy

SI8 is an independent commercial clearance reviewer — similar in posture to an audit firm, a technical standards assessor, or a professional certifier. The customer experience should reflect that positioning: rigorous but not adversarial, independent but not cold, evidence-based but not mechanical.

The customer's primary anxiety is uncertainty: uncertainty about whether their AI-generated content is commercially defensible, uncertainty about what evidence they need, and uncertainty about what a gap in documentation actually means for their campaign or their client relationship. SI8's job is to convert that uncertainty into specific, actionable commercial clarity — regardless of whether the outcome is fully positive.

A customer who receives a report with identified evidence gaps and conditions should leave the interaction more confident than when they arrived — because they now know specifically what the gaps are, what they mean commercially, and what to do about them. Clarity is the product, regardless of outcome.

## 3. Experience Principles

These principles guide every customer-facing interaction, communication, and artifact.

| # | Principle | In Practice |
|---|-----------|-------------|
| P1 | **Reduce uncertainty at every touchpoint.** | Customers should always know what happens next, what they need to do, and what SI8 is doing. |
| P2 | **Never shame a customer for missing evidence.** | Missing evidence is a finding. It is not a failure. The assessment is designed to handle it. |
| P3 | **Explain before requesting.** | Before asking a customer to provide something, explain what it is and why it matters. |
| P4 | **Evidence before opinion.** | SI8 evaluates evidence. SI8 does not render legal opinions. Every communication should reinforce this distinction. |
| P5 | **Professional but approachable.** | The tone is that of a trusted professional adviser — never cold, never casual. |
| P6 | **Independent but collaborative.** | SI8 is a reviewer, not an adversary. The relationship is collaborative within an independent structure. |
| P7 | **Every interaction should increase commercial confidence.** | Even a difficult finding should leave the customer more equipped to manage commercial risk than before. |
| P8 | **Leave every customer better prepared for their next AI campaign.** | The assessment is a learning experience, not just a transaction. |
| P9 | **Calibrate, do not alarm.** | Commercial risk is real but manageable. SI8 communicates risk accurately and specifically — not in ways that create disproportionate anxiety. |
| P10 | **Every evidence gap is a finding, not a failure.** | This is the single most important principle to reinforce throughout the customer experience. |

## 4. Primary Customer Personas

### Persona A — Agency Creative or Production Lead (ICP 1)

**Who they are:** Creative Director, Head of Production, or senior producer at a boutique or mid-size agency. Their clients include brands in regulated sectors (finserv, insurance, healthcare) where legal approval is part of the campaign sign-off process.

**Their situation:** Their agency has started using AI video tools in production. The work is good. The client's legal team is starting to ask questions. They don't have answers. A campaign is at risk of being held up or rejected without documentation they don't know how to produce.

**Their primary anxiety:** "I can't explain where this video came from, and I can't tell my client it's safe to use."

**What success looks like for them:** A professional document they can show their client's legal team that explains what evidence exists and what it means for commercial deployment. A clear statement of what to do if something is missing.

---

### Persona B — Line Producer or Executive Producer, Film/TV (ICP 2)

**Who they are:** Line Producer or EP on a scripted project that used AI tools in production. They are approaching the E&O insurance gate, or their distributor has asked for documentation.

**Their situation:** They need Chain of Title documentation to proceed to distribution or to satisfy an E&O underwriter. They have never produced this documentation for AI-generated content before.

**Their primary anxiety:** "I don't know what the insurance company or distributor needs, and I can't deliver the project without it."

**What success looks like for them:** A structured document that satisfies the documentation request — or, if evidence gaps exist, a clear statement of what is missing and how to address it before the insurance or distribution gate.

---

### Persona C — Brand or Holdco Legal / Governance (ICP 3 — Future)

*Not a primary target in Year 1. Defined here for future planning.*

**Who they are:** General Counsel, IP Counsel, or AI Governance Lead at a brand, holdco, or platform. They are setting internal standards for AI content approval.

**Their situation:** They need to establish what documentation standard they will require from agencies submitting AI-generated creative.

**What success looks like for them:** SI8 becomes the pre-approved standard that agencies are required to meet before submitting AI creative for brand approval.

## 6. Emotional Journey

The intended emotional arc across the full customer journey.

```
Awareness          →  Initial Outreach      →  Evidence Prep       →  Submission
Uncertain              Cautious but curious     Motivated              Anxious

Assessment         →  Report Delivery       →  Follow-Up           →  Future Readiness
Waiting                Attentive              Clarifying             Confident, prepared
```

**The ideal arc:**

> Uncertainty → Reassurance → Engagement → Patience → Clarity → Confidence → Preparation

At no point should the customer feel:
- Judged for what they don't have
- Confused about what happens next
- Unsure about what the report means for their actual deployment decision
- Abandoned after the report is delivered

## 7. What SI8 Should Communicate at Each Stage

| Stage | Communicate |
|-------|-------------|
| Awareness | "There is a real, growing risk in AI video commercial deployment — and a professional standard for addressing it." |
| Initial Outreach | "We review the commercial evidence behind AI-generated content. Here's what that covers and what the deliverable looks like." |
| Qualification | "Here's whether this is a good fit for your situation, and why. Here's what the assessment cannot do." |
| Pre-Sale Evidence Preparation | "Bring what you have. You probably have more than you think. Missing items are handled in the assessment, not used to disqualify." |
| Purchase / Commitment | "You've purchased an SI8 Certified review. Here is exactly what that includes, when you'll receive it, and what you need to do now." |
| Evidence Submission | "Submit what you have. Every gap you describe becomes part of the assessment, not a problem we set aside." |
| Assessment In Progress | "Your review is underway. Five business days from submission. We'll reach out if we have a question." |
| Report Delivery | "Here is what the evidence shows. Here is what it means for your deployment. Here are the residual risks to monitor. Here is what to do next." |
| Follow-Up / Clarification | "We can explain any finding in plain English. Here's what [X finding] means and why it matters." |
| Future Campaign Readiness | "Here's what made this submission strong. Here's what to improve for next time. We're here when you're ready." |

## 8. What SI8 Should Avoid at Each Stage

| Stage | Avoid |
|-------|-------|
| Awareness | Alarmism. Overclaiming risk. Making prospective customers feel they are already in trouble. |
| Initial Outreach | Jargon. Legal-sounding language that feels threatening. Vague or overpromising outcomes. |
| Qualification | Pressure. Vague commitments. "We can probably help" without specifics. |
| Pre-Sale Evidence Preparation | Making incomplete evidence sound like a disqualifier. Presenting the checklist as a barrier to pass. |
| Purchase / Commitment | Small-print shock. Confusing process steps. Loss of momentum after payment. |
| Evidence Submission | Conveying that the CertForm is an audit. Making the customer feel they are being interrogated. |
| Assessment In Progress | Radio silence. No acknowledgment of progress. Letting the customer wonder if something went wrong. |
| Report Delivery | Binary pass/fail language. Jargon. Findings that imply legal determination. Overpromising on what the report enables. |
| Follow-Up / Clarification | Defensiveness about findings. Suggesting the customer should have provided more evidence. Retreating from independent assessment under pushback. |
| Future Campaign Readiness | Forgetting the customer exists after delivery. No re-engagement. No forward path. |

## 9. Success Criteria

A successful SI8 customer experience produces the following outcomes:

**Minimum:**
- Customer understands what they received and what it does and does not mean for their deployment decision.
- Customer can articulate: what evidence was reviewed, what gaps were identified, and what the overall commercial confidence assessment is.
- Customer does not feel that SI8 judged them for missing evidence.
- Customer feels the engagement was worth $499.

**Target:**
- Customer is better prepared for their next AI campaign than they were before the assessment.
- Customer is confident they can show the report to a client legal team or E&O underwriter.
- Customer would commission a second assessment without hesitation.

**Aspirational:**
- Customer recommends SI8 to another agency or producer without being asked.
- Customer begins treating SI8 as the standard pre-deployment step for all AI campaign work.

## 10. Future Experience Improvements

The following improvements are not currently operational but would materially improve the customer experience as SI8 scales.

| Improvement | Stage | Current State | Why It Matters |
|-------------|-------|---------------|----------------|
| Automated Engagement Confirmation Email | Purchase / Commitment | Manual send by JD | Delays post-purchase reassurance; inconsistent delivery |
| Submission confirmation email with evidence review tips | Evidence Submission | Not implemented | Reduces anxiety during the submission process; reinforces incomplete-evidence-is-normal message |
| Assessment progress communication (mid-review) | Assessment In Progress | No touchpoint | Radio silence during review is the highest anxiety period; even one update reduces churn risk |
| Structured report walkthrough call option | Report Delivery | Not offered | Complex findings benefit from live explanation; increases confidence in the report and in SI8 |
| Future campaign checklist in report | Future Campaign Readiness | Not in current Report Template | Directly extends value of the report beyond the immediate assessment |
| Re-engagement sequence (6-month trigger) | Future Campaign Readiness | Not implemented | Converts one-time customers into repeat buyers |
| Customer portal with submission history | All stages | Not built | Gives customers visibility into their own assessment track record |
