## Prompting Philosophy for This Project

The brief required visuals that felt premium and aspirational without using real people. My prompting strategy across all sessions followed three rules:

1. **No identifiable humans.** All prompts explicitly excluded faces or identifiable features. Where people were included (e.g., Scene 4, over-shoulder shot), the framing ensured no face was visible.
2. **Brand colour consistency.** Harborne's palette is amber/navy/white. From Session 6 onward, I added colour palette specifications to all prompts.
3. **Cinematic motion.** All prompts included "cinematic" and specified motion characteristics (slow drift, minimal movement) to suit the campaign's confident, unhurried tone.
