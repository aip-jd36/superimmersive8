## LinkedIn Post — Draft A (cost progression angle — recommended)

We spent $14.19 to get one reply from a potential customer. Now we're spending $1.39.

That's not a copywriting win. It's 90 days of being wrong about who our customer was.

March 2026: 298 LinkedIn messages into Sydney, targeting business owners and marketing consultants with a pitch about an AI film catalog. 3 replies. The problem wasn't the copy. It was the country, the title, and the product that didn't exist yet.

We rebuilt from there. Scrapped the catalog model entirely. Switched to a verification service — $499 for a Chain of Title review on AI video. Then started testing messages. Legal Friction outperformed everything: one question, sent cold, that either lands immediately or doesn't land at all. "When you deliver AI-generated video to a client, are their legal teams asking for documentation?"

Same question sent to Marketing Managers in London: $2.03 per reply. Still wrong person.

Same question sent to Creative Directors who had AI video in their LinkedIn profile: $1.36 per reply in England. $0.88 in Dubai.

Dubai wasn't in the plan. One campaign, 388 contacts, 54% acceptance rate, 16.2% response rate. The market is earlier than London — creative directors there are producing AI video at volume but the compliance conversation hasn't caught up yet. When you ask the right question to the right person before their market has figured out the answer, they want to talk.

6,391 outreaches. 9 geos. 23 campaigns. The ICP on day one was a guess. The ICP now is a cost table.

Most failed outreach is diagnosed as a messaging problem. In our case it was a list problem every time.

## LinkedIn Post — Draft B (shorter, punchier)

$14.19 to generate one LinkedIn reply. That was March 2026.

$1.39 today.

It's not a better email. It's three things that changed: the person we're talking to, the question we're asking, and the geography.

Wrong person (Owner+, Marketing Manager) with any message: $3-14 per reply.
Right person (Creative Director, AI Video) with wrong message: still $3+.
Right person, right question, right geo (Dubai): $0.88 per response, $1.39 per warm reply.

We ran 23 campaigns across 9 geographies over 90 days to find that. The data is in a spreadsheet now. Next budget decision is just reading it from the bottom up.
