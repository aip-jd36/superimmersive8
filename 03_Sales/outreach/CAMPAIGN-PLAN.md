## Campaign Framework — Three Tracks

SI8 is now running three structurally different campaign types in parallel. They target different layers of the B2B2B chain and should be measured differently.

| Track | ICP | Purpose | Measure by | Hook |
|-------|-----|---------|------------|------|
| **Track A — Creative Directors** | Agency CD / Senior Producer / Founder | Sales — direct | Warm reply rate, sample requests, call rate | EU AI Act urgency (through Aug 2); Legal Friction (evergreen) |
| **Track B — Business Affairs / Clearance-Pro** | Head of BA, Broadcast Affairs, EP, Line Producer, HoP at agencies + production cos | Sales — direct to operational buyer | Warm reply rate, workflow fit confirmation | Client-demand probe — no EU Act framing needed |
| **Track C — Brand Legal Teams** | In-house IP Counsel, Brand Legal, Holdco AI Governance | Discovery — validate B2B2B chain, Test 8 upside | Reply quality, B2B2B chain descriptions, pre-approval conversations | Regulatory event hooks (ASA, NY law, EU Act, GEMA) |

**How they interact:** Track C (Brand Legal) validates the top of the chain. Track B (Business Affairs) is the operational buyer who receives the requirement. Track A (Creative Directors) responds fastest but refers up to BA for procurement. CD warm leads may become internal referral paths to BA — not end buyers.

**Post-August 2 note:** EU Act urgency hook in Track A loses force after August 2. Legal Friction sequence is the evergreen fallback. Plan a hook refresh for September.
