## Strategic Logic

Agency campaigns (V1) target the agency feeling the pain. Legal team campaigns flip the model:
- **Who we're talking to:** Agency GCs, Holdco AI Governance leads, Brand IP/Legal Counsel
- **Why:** These are the people who WRITE the requirement ("no AI video without Chain of Title documentation") that the agency must then comply with
- **The B2B2B outcome:** Legal team pre-approves SI8's format → tells their agencies "use this vendor" → agencies come to SI8 inbound
- **Analogy:** Getting PCI DSS to mandate a security vendor. Getting E&O underwriters to require a clearance process.
