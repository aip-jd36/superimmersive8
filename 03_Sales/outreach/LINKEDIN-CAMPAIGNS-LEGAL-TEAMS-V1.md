# LinkedIn Outbound Campaigns — Legal Team Discovery
**Written:** June 7, 2026
**Updated:** June 7, 2026 — Restructured as discovery-first campaigns (no pitch, pure validation)
**Model:** Discovery → validate what brand legal teams need before building sales campaigns
**Tool:** Dripify — separate from agency campaigns
**Status:** Draft — ready to load into Dripify

## Purpose

These are NOT sales campaigns. Goal: get brand legal teams and holdco governance leads to describe what they currently require from agencies for AI video approval — and whether any format would satisfy that requirement. The intelligence from these conversations shapes the product and the next wave of sales campaigns.

**What we're validating:**
- Is blocking of AI video campaigns by brand legal teams real and widespread?
- What specifically do they ask agencies to produce?
- Would a structured Chain of Title document satisfy that requirement?
- What would need to change about our format for them to accept it?

**How discovery messaging differs from sales:**
- No product mention in Message 1 or 2
- Message 1 ends with a yes/no question — no time ask
- Time ask (meeting) doesn't appear until Message 4
- Success = a reply describing their current process, not a demo booked
