## Scoring key

RSE = Regulated-Sector Exposure, IAE = Institutional Approval Exposure. Each 0 (none) / 1 (weak — general claim, no named client) / 2 (confirmed — named client + case study) / 3 (strong — multiple, marquee name, or award-recognized). Class: HGE if RSE≥2 or IAE≥2; SCE if RSE≤1 and IAE≤1. Confidence: Verified (agency's own case study or named-client trade citation) / Network-inferred / Directory-only.

**PYR-U vs. PYR-C:** Person Yield Ratio comes in two forms, not interchangeable. **PYR-U** (uncapped) = estimated eligible people ÷ agencies, no extraction limit — used for UK and Germany, sourced before the per-agency cap existed. **PYR-C** (capped) = same calculation with per-agency extraction capped at 8 (Sampling Methodology v1.1 Question 4) — used for Netherlands onward. PYR-C will structurally read lower than PYR-U for HGE-heavy arms (high-density agencies are held at 8 instead of their full 10+ estimate) — a lower PYR-C is not evidence of a smaller effect, it's evidence the cap is doing its job. Do not compare PYR-U and PYR-C figures directly without noting which is which.
