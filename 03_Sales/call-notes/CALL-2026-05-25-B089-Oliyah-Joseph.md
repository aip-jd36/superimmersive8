## Summary

Oliyah connected via LinkedIn (Lilly/Ivy Legal Friction campaign). Call was with both Oliyah and her husband Paul, who handles client relationships and is also an AI content creator. ## Key Signals

- **Medium mismatch:** AI images only, not video. Platform is built for video provenance — images are a much lower-risk surface.
- **Client tier mismatch:** SMB/e-commerce. No legal teams on client side. Documentation requests not on their radar.
- **No active pain:** "Never been asked for any documentation." Current protection = NDA + "please don't post our product on social."
- **Original assets only:** Paul: "We're creating from scratch — no named likenesses, no product IP references. It's just common sense."
- **Tools used:** Kling ("Clean"), plus several general AI image platforms. Multiple tools depending on output needed.
- **Paul's objection (key):** "Anybody can sign a bit of paper — how legal is the document you sign off on anyway?" → Wants third-party legal standing, not just a structured PDF.
- **Paul's validation:** "I think you are playing ahead. Maybe just a longer time to reap the fruits. Give it another 6 months, maybe, it's getting quite chaotic."
- **EU AI Act awareness:** Paul aware of regulatory direction but doesn't feel pressure yet. Sees it as "future" not "now."
- **Coupon offer:** JD offered free comp codes. Paul said "thank you" but didn't commit — effectively a soft decline.

## Objections Heard

1. **"Is it even legally valid?"** — Paul questioned whether the Chain of Title PDF has actual legal standing vs. being a well-formatted document anyone could produce. JD clarified SI8 is a structured audit trail / third-party validator, not an insurance company.
2. **"We're not being asked for this."** — No client demand signal yet. They're pre-pain.
3. **"It's extra work and extra cost on top of creators."** — Paul sees compliance documentation as a burden, not a service. This framing is a sales challenge for the early-adopter pitch.
4. **"This is more for the future."** — Genuine belief the need isn't here yet for their specific client tier.
