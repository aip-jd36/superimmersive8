## Point 3 — Industries moving fastest

**Share your take, then invite his:**

Industries moving fastest:
- **Financial services** — clearest signal in every geo. UnaFinancial is exactly this. Brand legal teams at banks/fintechs most risk-averse; pushing requirements to agencies first
- **Government / public sector** — procurement requirements stricter than commercial; AI content slots into existing compliance frameworks
- **Pharma / healthcare** — regulated sector with existing content compliance; AI layer slots into a process they already have
- **Premium retail / luxury brands** — brand equity risk more than regulation (Barilla, Bloomingdale's-type clients already asking)
- **Streaming platforms** — Netflix/Amazon/Apple require E&O insurance minimums currently unavailable for uncleared AI productions; forces the issue for film/TV

Industries slowest: general creative agencies, SMBs, non-regulated sectors.

**The mechanism to explain:**
> "It's less about regulation and more about regulated-sector client exposure. Brand legal at a bank has existing compliance infrastructure — AI content just creates a new category they need to clear. Once one bank requires it from one agency, that agency requires it from all subcontractors. Spreads by client pressure, not by law."

**Then invite his take:**
> "From where you sit at UnaFinancial — is the compliance pressure coming from internal risk teams, or from external partners and clients you work with? Curious which direction it moves from in your environment."
