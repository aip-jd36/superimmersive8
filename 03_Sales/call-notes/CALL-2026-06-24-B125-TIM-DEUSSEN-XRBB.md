## Post-Call Product Implications — C2PA Microservice Architecture

*Developed Jun 24, 2026 post-call, based on Tim's C2PA/interoperability guidance.*

Tim's call surfaced a specific product architecture question: if SI8 tracks what needs to be disclosed legally, can it also connect to the technical tools that embed the disclosure in the video file?

**The answer is yes — and the plumbing already exists.**

### Three-Layer Stack

| Layer | What | Who |
|-------|------|-----|
| **1. Labeling/Disclosure** | EU AI Act Art. 50, NY Synthetic Performer Law — flag as AI-generated | C2PA standard (machine-readable) + human-readable caption/watermark |
| **2. Commercial Clearance** | IP status, training data licensing, likeness assessment, commercial use authorization | **SI8 — the judgment layer** |
| **3. Integration / Embedding** | Embeds SI8's clearance output as a C2PA custom assertion in the video file | Truepic API, Capture REST API, open-source c2pa-rs SDK |

### C2PA Custom Assertions

C2PA supports custom assertions beyond its 20 standard fields. SI8's clearance data can be embedded directly in the C2PA manifest:

```json
{
  "SI8:clearance_status": "CLEARED",
  "SI8:chain_of_title_id": "SI8-2026-004921",
  "SI8:tools_verified": ["Runway Gen-3", "Kling 1.6"],
  "SI8:licensing_confirmed": true,
  "SI8:likeness_assessment": "no synthetic performers identified",
  "SI8:commercial_use_authorized": true,
  "SI8:disclosure_required": ["EU AI Act Art. 50", "NY Synthetic Performer Law - N/A"],
  "SI8:review_date": "2026-06-24",
  "SI8:reviewer": "SI8 certified human reviewer"
}
```

The video file carries SI8's clearance data cryptographically — tamper-evident, platform-verifiable.

### Capture Compliance Confirmation (Jun 24, 2026 — same session)

*Source: captureapp.xyz/compliance/ — reviewed same session.*

**1. Multi-layer is legally required, not optional.**
EU Code of Practice states: "no single active marking technique suffices." C2PA alone (in-file) = insufficient. Watermark alone = insufficient. The legally endorsed architecture is: C2PA in file + ERC-7053 content hash registered on-chain. If either layer survives, the content is provably AI-generated.

**2. ERC-7053 on-chain solves the stripping problem.**
C2PA is stripped during post-production export or platform re-upload. The ERC-7053 on-chain registration stores a hash of the content at signing time. Even after stripping, a third-party auditor can verify the file against the on-chain record — no file contact required. This makes the compliance durable.

**3. Capture targets AI model providers — the agency delivery gap is unaddressed.**
Capture's compliance framing: "C2PA credentials embedded at generation time." Case studies: Reuters, Starling Lab, generative AI companies (Runway-type inference pipeline integration). No agency, creative director, or ad production workflow is mentioned. The gap SI8 fills — re-signing the final composited campaign video at the delivery step — is explicitly outside Capture's current market positioning.

**4. August 2, 2026 enforcement is real and imminent.**
39 days from this session. Maximum fine: €15M or 3% of global annual turnover. Integration time via Capture: 1–2 weeks. Agencies that can't answer yes to Capture's compliance checklist are currently exposed.
