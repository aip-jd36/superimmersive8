## What Alice Shared (Private 1:1 Conversation, Later That Evening)

**The pain point (validates SI8's core thesis, agency-side):** Several of Anchor's large Taiwan enterprise clients are becoming hesitant to approve AI-generated commercials because they don't know whether the content is commercially safe to use. Close match to SI8's own framing of the problem.

**The evidence gap (a real, self-disclosed admission):** Anchor has not consistently preserved original prompts or production logs on past AI projects. In some cases the team has had to "recreate" prompts after the fact. Alice herself recognizes that reconstructed prompts aren't reliable provenance and don't accurately prove how content was made — this is, unprompted, the exact distinction behind SI8's Domain H corroboration rule (a detailed account without a contemporaneous artifact is a claim, not evidence).

**Origin of the "Creation Dossier Platform" concept:** After visiting NAB (trade show) and seeing a product called "Liquid Engine," Alice decided to develop a grant proposal for an internal AI creation-dossier / prompt-logging platform — capturing production logs, generating C2PA credentials, creating QR-verifiable records, on-chain notarization. The deck names Numbers Protocol as the intended provenance/metadata partner. This origin story is a specific, checkable detail (NAB + a named competing/adjacent product) that adds credibility to "proposal, not yet built" beyond just Alice's word — see the evidentiary caveat below.

## Important Evidentiary Distinction

The deck shows a detailed system architecture but does not itself prove the platform is built, funded, or operational — confirmed by direct review (see `CRM.md` B164 entry for the full verification trail, including the cross-check against Anchor's page-10 list of already-secured 2025 grants, which does not name this platform). Alice's direct statement that it's currently a grant proposal is credible first-party information, not independently corroborated by the deck text itself. Treat as: JD's direct-conversation account, reinforced by a specific and checkable origin story, not a deck-verified fact.

**On methodology overlap — related but not identical:** the deck's stated principle ("only human originality has IP rights, hence the creation dossier") is related to SI8's corroboration requirement, but the systems do different jobs. Anchor's proposed system is designed to **generate and preserve first-party production evidence** (the raw material). SI8's role would be to **independently evaluate what that evidence actually supports** (the judgment layer). These are complementary functions, not duplicate ones — this is the basis for the "not currently competitive" classification.
