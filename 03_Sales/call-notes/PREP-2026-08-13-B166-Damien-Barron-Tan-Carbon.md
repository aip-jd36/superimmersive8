Following the CRC Phase 3 design discipline built for exactly this kind of conversation: open with process language, never introduce "legal," "compliance," "procurement," "risk," or "audit" unprompted — let Damien bring those words in if they're real for him.

**Opening (project/workflow, not commercial-context yet):**

**Process/handoff (this is the Phase 3 pattern, verbatim in spirit):**

**Only if he brings it up himself:**
