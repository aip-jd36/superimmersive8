## 5. ICP Thesis (as of 2026-05-26)

**Primary ICP — Boutique Founder / Creative Director at AI Production Studio:**
- 1-20 person studio producing AI video for brand/agency clients
- Has client-facing relationships where legal or procurement review is beginning
- Founder/owner or senior creative — can buy $499 unilaterally
- Based in UK, Germany, or Dubai
- Best conversion path: direct reply from JD + sample + 15-min call

**Secondary ICP — Senior Video/Production Specialist at Holdco Arm:**
- Matthew Sergison-Main (Brandtech) is the archetype
- Fielding real client requests for AI documentation in their day job
- "Gen AI Specialist" or "Senior Production Specialist" titles at network agencies
- Longer sales cycle but potential for volume accounts ($499 × multiple verifications/month)
- Best entry: probe call + platform walk-through + reference to IAB/ASA framework

**The Sweet Spot Profile (unchanged from May 24, now confirmed by Oliyah call):**
Someone who has already improvised a compliance process — prompt logs, metadata folders, email trails — and wants a standardized output they can hand to clients. This is validated by call data: Paul (Oliyah's husband) explicitly said "I put it on my desktop" as the current workaround. That workflow = SI8's primary buyer.

**What the Oliyah call confirmed:**
- AI images → NOT a fit. AI video is the right wedge — images carry far less perceived legal exposure.
- SMB clients with no legal teams → NOT a fit. The ICP trigger is "my client has a legal team that reviews campaigns."
- The refined ICP filter: "Does your client have a legal team that reviews AI campaign assets?" — not just "do you use AI?"
