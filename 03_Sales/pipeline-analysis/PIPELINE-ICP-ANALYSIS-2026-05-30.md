## 5. ICP Thesis

**Primary ICP (confirmed, tightened):**
Boutique Founder / Creative Director / Senior Production Specialist at an AI video studio or agency (5–50 people), producing content for named brand clients whose legal teams apply compliance pressure. Geo: UK first, Germany close second, Dubai third. Can buy $499 without internal approval. Fastest conversion path: Trigger 1 pain-mirror → free code or 20-min call depending on signal depth.

**Secondary ICP (emerging — not yet closed a deal):**
Senior Production Specialist or CD *inside* a holdco execution arm (OLIVER, Monks, JvM, McCann). Same pain profile as boutique, but absorbed from above rather than from clients directly. Higher value if they convert (agency-wide agreements possible) but longer cycle — may need internal champion or director sign-off. Matthew Sergison-Main (B088) is the archetype.

**The sweet spot profile (fastest to convert):**
Trigger 1 Founder at a 10–30 person AI video studio, UK or Germany, with named brand client relationships. They have the pain, the authority to buy, and no approval chain. Ramez Tabshi (B100, June 1 call) and Florent Delavous (B095) are the closest current examples.

**Trigger 3 as a strategic outlier:**
Christopher Neitzert (B128), Florent Delavous (B095), and Gabriel Preston (B121) are not standard ICP. They've already validated the problem themselves by building a workaround. These leads could be: (a) buyers who want to upgrade their informal process to something defensible, (b) potential partners whose solution is complementary to SI8, or (c) competitors. Needs a compare-notes conversation before classifying.

## 6. Targeting Implications

1. **Increase Germany campaign volume immediately.** 4/4 warm leads, 100% current-pain signal, EU Act enforcement creating organic urgency. The Berlin campaign at modest volume is outperforming UK on warm rate. Run at full UK-equivalent volume.

2. **Launch Singapore as a primary geo campaign.** 42% warm rate in May 29 discovery pool vs 6% for UK. Current pipeline has zero Singapore warm leads — which means the campaign volume and/or follow-up isn't matching the signal. Dedicate equal Lilly/Ivy capacity to Singapore as to UK.

3. **Add "Senior Production Specialist" and "Head of Production" as explicit title targets** alongside Creative Director and Founder. The OLIVER archetype (holdco execution layer) is a distinct and high-value profile now confirmed in the pipeline.

4. **Add "AI" as a positive title keyword in list-building.** "AI Video Producer," "Gen AI Specialist," "AI Creative Director" = front of queue. Confirmed across analyses.

5. **Design a Trigger 3 outreach arm.** Current messaging (discovery question + Legal Friction hook) isn't the right entry point for leads who've already built a process. Message for this group: *"You're clearly already tracking this — what we do is turn your existing process into a document that holds up in a formal review. Worth comparing notes?"*

6. **Probe holdco leads explicitly for volume.** When Monks (B122), JvM (B126), or McCann (B002) engage, ask: *"Is this something your team handles project by project, or does the whole agency need a consistent format?"* That's the entry point to a recurring agency agreement.

## 7. What is NOT Working

**Amsterdam / Netherlands — warm conversion problem.** Despite strong campaign response rates and multiple followed-up leads (B062–B075 in CRM), only one warm lead (B138 Tunç Topçuoglu) has surfaced. NL leads describe informal processes and say "we manage it" — they don't feel active legal pressure yet. The market may be ahead of itself in self-organization but behind in formal compliance requirements. Consider pausing NL volume expansion until Tunç converts or another warm signal emerges.

**VP+ and C-suite titles.** Not a single CMO, VP Marketing, or CCO has converted to warm lead. The pain lives at the execution layer (CD, production specialist, founder). Strategy titles acknowledge the problem as "coming" but aren't in pain today.

**Pure freelancers without agency clients.** Several freelancers replied with "I just disclose it" or "I own everything because I prompted it." Without a client legal team applying external pressure, there's no urgency. Filter these out at list-building stage.

**Calendly-first approach for holdco leads.** Luke Brady (B043, Sublime Animations) has missed calls three times. The "offer slots" → no-show pattern is a friction signal — suggesting that for some leads, a Calendly link is too high-commitment. Free code may be a better first ask, with call reserved for post-activation.
