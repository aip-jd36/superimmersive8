## 5. ICP Thesis — Updated 2026-06-16

**Primary ICP (unchanged from May):**
Boutique Founder or Creative Director at an AI production studio, 1-20 people, producing AI video for brand/agency clients. Based in UK, Germany, or Dubai. Client legal teams are starting to ask questions. Founder can buy $499 without internal approval.

**Secondary ICP (unchanged):**
Senior Production Specialist or Gen AI Specialist at a holdco arm (OLIVER, Monks, JvM, McCann units). Already fielding live client requests. Longer sales cycle (3-6 months) but higher LTV and potential for multi-submission volume deals.

**NEW — Emerging ICP #3: AI Compliance Advisor**
Freelance AI Creative Director who advises 5-15 brands simultaneously on AI content strategy and compliance. Not running a studio. Client-embedded. When they adopt SI8, each of their client engagements becomes a potential submission. Simon Helm is the prototype. Tim Deussen (XRBB) is adjacent — regulatory insider rather than an advisor, but same category of "one person, multiple client touchpoints."

**The Sweet Spot (confirmed):**
A lead who has already improvised a compliance process — prompt logs, email trails, metadata folders — and wants to replace it with a standardized deliverable they can send to client legal teams. This describes the 5 highest-signal leads across all geos: Matthew Sergison-Main, Ibrahim Badi, Gabriel Preston, Sultan Alsuwaidi, Daniele Zennaro.
