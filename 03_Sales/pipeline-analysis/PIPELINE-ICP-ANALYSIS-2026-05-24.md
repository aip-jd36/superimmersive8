## 5. ICP Thesis (as of 2026-05-24)

**Primary ICP — Boutique Founder / CD at AI Production Studio:**
- 1-20 person studio producing 100% AI video for brand clients
- Has client-facing relationships with legal or procurement
- Founder/owner or senior creative — can buy $499 unilaterally
- Based in UK, Germany, or Dubai

**Secondary ICP — Creative/Production Lead at Holdco Arm:**
- Senior specialist at an agency network arm (OLIVER, Monks, JvM, McCann units)
- Already fielding client questions about AI documentation
- Longer sales cycle (3-6 months) but potential for volume
- The "Gen AI Specialist" title is the entry point

**The Sweet Spot Profile:**
Someone who has already improvised a compliance process (prompt logs, metadata, informal email trails) and wants a standardized output to hand to clients. This describes the highest-signal leads across all geos: Gabriel Preston, Matthew Sergison-Main, Ibrahim Badi, Sultan Alsuwaidi.

## 7. What is NOT a Good ICP Signal

- Brand-side people (Philip Morris, GEMS World Academy) — end up as research/intelligence, not buyers
- Academic / institutional roles — research, not buyers
- VP / SVP at large corps — enterprise buying process too slow for Year 1
- Hybrid-only producers (AI used internally, not in final output) — wrong workflow
