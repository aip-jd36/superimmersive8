**The Sweet Spot (unchanged):**
Someone who has already improvised a compliance process — prompt logs, platform lists, metadata docs, email trails — and wants a structured output they can hand to clients or attach to deliverables. They have validated the problem themselves. SI8 pitch: "You've done the hard part. We turn what you've built into a client-presentable Chain of Title." ### B. Three-Track B2B2B Model — Current Assessment

**Source:** Campaign Intelligence Report Jun 9 + Daniele Zennaro (B145) Jun 10 + `03_Sales/outreach/CAMPAIGN-INTELLIGENCE-REPORT-SE-2026-06-09.md`

The core insight from the last two weeks of testing: **Creative Directors are the wrong end-buyer.** They respond to outreach and acknowledge the problem, but based on Daniele Zennaro's B2B2B probe reply, the actual documentation requirement flows through a different function:

> Brand Legal → **Commercial/Rights Clearance (Business Affairs)** → Creative/Production

The three-track model is the structural response to this:

| Track | ICP | Role in chain | Proven? | Campaign |
|-------|-----|---------------|---------|----------|
| **Track A** | Creative Director / Senior Producer | *Outreach entry point* — replies to outreach, surfaces pain, potential internal champion. Not the procurement decision-maker. | ✅ **Confirmed** — 30% warm rate across 417 responses (Campaign Intelligence Report Jun 9) | Legal Friction — keep running |
| **Track B** | Business Affairs / Broadcast Affairs / EP / Line Producer / HoP | *Operational buyer* — holds clearance budget, manages clearance suppliers, receives documentation requirements from brand legal and procures solutions | ❌ **Unproven** — Test 9 not yet launched; hypothesis based on single Daniele Zennaro signal | Clearance-Pro-v1 — needs UK BA target list |
| **Track C** | Brand Legal / In-house IP Counsel / Holdco AI Governance | *Originator* — the source of the documentation requirement; if they pre-approve SI8's format, it creates pull toward SI8 at agencies | ⚠️ **Directional** — ~80% warm rate but n~10; Who's Asking campaign active but 0 connections yet | Who's Asking, NY 1st Law — discovery only |

**What the 30% warm rate from C2 (Track A) tells us:**

The Campaign Intelligence Report confirmed the Legal Friction sequence on Creative Directors produces a 30% warm rate — the strongest single metric in the dataset. This does NOT mean CDs are the wrong ICP. It means they are the right **first contact** who can either (a) be the buyer at boutique studios where the CD IS the business affairs function, or (b) be the internal referral path to Business Affairs at larger agencies. Both are valid outcomes from Track A outreach.

**The distinction that matters:**

| Company size | Who is the buyer | Track A outcome |
|---|---|---|
| Boutique studio (1–10 people) | The CD is also the BA — same person makes all procurement decisions | Track A → direct close |
| Mid-size agency (10–50 people) | Separate BA function | Track A → internal referral to BA → Track B closes |
| Holdco arm / large agency | Dedicated BA team + legal review | Track A identifies the pain; Track B + C close |

This explains why no Track A lead has converted to a verified submission yet: at the companies where the CD IS the buyer (boutique), they've been engaged but not pitched to close; at larger agencies, the CD can't close without BA involvement, and BA hasn't been contacted.
