## Key Insights

**The CreaDir_AI Video target is the strongest segment.**
Top 4 campaigns by response rate all use this targeting. Mgr+/VP+/CMO targeting consistently underperforms (2-7% response vs 9-16% for CreaDir).

**Dubai is the standout geo.**
54% acceptance rate (more than double any other geo) + 16.3% response rate. Only 1 campaign run. Significant headroom to scale.

**England (CreaDir) vs London (Mgr+/VP+) is the clearest A/B test.**
England_0426A_IL: 14% response. London Mgr+ campaigns: 2-7% response. Same alias (Ivy), same region, different targeting. CreaDir wins decisively.

**Singapore connects but doesn't respond.**
28% acceptance rate (good) but 4.9% response rate (poor). Connects readily, doesn't engage. Pain not felt yet — market 6-12 months behind UK/Dubai.

**Sydney: dead geo. Do not pursue.**
16.1% acceptance, 1.0% response. Lowest of all geos. No follow-up campaigns planned.

**Berlin: promising but sample too small.**
364 leads, 9.3% response — comparable to top UK campaigns. Only 1 campaign. Scale up to validate.

**Amsterdam: lower acceptance but good response quality.**
26.3% acceptance, 11.7% response — 4th best response rate. 8 warm leads followed up May 19. Next Amsterdam batch should test different messaging timing to improve acceptance rate.
