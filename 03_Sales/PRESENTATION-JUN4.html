02 · The Problem

The most expensive mistake a B2B founder makes is pitching to the wrong person for 6 months.

Option A

Cold email

People only reply if they want to buy. No PMF yet = no replies.

Circular. You need the answer to get the answer.
Option B

Focus groups

8–10 participants, professional moderator, facility.

$4,000–$12,000 · 2–4 weeks to recruit
Option C

Customer interviews

45 min × 10 calls = 2 weeks scheduling. Still need the right people.

$100–$300/participant to recruit
"All three assume you already know who your customer is."
