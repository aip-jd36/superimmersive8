SI8's approach

Technical provenance can document assertions about a file's origin and history. Commercial readiness is a different question.

Technical provenance

Standards such as C2PA and Content Credentials can carry signed assertions about an asset's origin and edit history. They are useful and increasingly common, but an assertion is only as reliable as its source, it does not establish commercial-use rights, and it may not survive downstream processing or transcoding.

Commercial readiness

Documentation and, where you need it, an independent human review of the evidence picture — licensing, IP, likeness, authorship. SI8 works at this layer.

What SI8 is — and is not.

  • Not legal advice, and not a legal opinion on copyright, trademark, or any other right.
  • Not a clearance, a certification, or a determination that a project can legally be used.
  • A Creator Record is self-declared; SI8 does not check your answers, and automated readings are not verification of them.
  • An automated reading reports what a document appears to show. It does not establish that a document is genuine.
  • Your regulatory obligations remain yours.
