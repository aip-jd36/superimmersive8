Website Privacy Notice

PMF Strategy Inc., doing business as SuperImmersive 8 ("SI8", "we" or "us")
Version website-privacy-2026-11-18 · Effective 18 November 2026

This notice explains how SI8 handles personal information on its website at www.superimmersive8.com. Creator Record and the Commercial Readiness Check, at app.superimmersive8.com, each have their own privacy notice. This notice does not cover them.

1. Information we collect

  • When you browse: technical information your browser sends, such as your IP address, browser and device type, and the pages you view. Our hosting provider receives this to serve the site, and Google receives it when the site loads its fonts and Google Analytics (section 3).
  • When you contact us: your name, work email, the inquiry type you choose and your message.

The website itself does not ask you to create an account or make a payment.

2. How we use it

  • To show you the website and keep it working.
  • To understand how the site is used (section 3).
  • To read and reply to your enquiry, and to keep a record of our correspondence.
  • To protect the contact form from automated and repeated submissions.

3. Analytics and cookies

The website uses Google Analytics to understand how it is used. Google Analytics loads when you open the site and uses cookies or similar technologies to collect technical information about your device, your browser and how you use the pages. The website does not provide a setting to turn it off. You can block analytics or cookies through your browser settings or a browser extension.

The website's own pages do not set other cookies.

4. Contact form

Your enquiry is sent to SI8 by email and kept in SI8's email inbox. The website does not keep a separate copy. Your email address is used as the reply address so that we can answer you.

To stop automated and repeated submissions, the form includes a hidden field and a minimum time before sending, and we limit how many enquiries can be sent from one connection in a short period. For that limit, a one-way code derived from your IP address is held briefly in the server's memory and is not stored.

Sending an enquiry does not add you to any mailing list.

5. Service providers

  • Vercel: website hosting, and running the contact form.
  • Google: fonts, Google Analytics, and SI8's email inbox, which receives enquiries.
  • Resend: delivering contact-form enquiries to SI8.

Links to sign-up, sign-in, the Commercial Readiness Check and call booking take you to services covered by their own notices and terms. These providers process information to provide their services to SI8. We do not sell your personal information.

6. Where information is processed

SI8 and its service providers may store and process information in countries other than the one where you live, including the United States.

7. How long we keep information

We do not currently delete information automatically. Enquiries are kept in SI8's email inbox until they are deleted. Copies may remain in our service providers' systems and backups until they are deleted or expire. We are reviewing our retention practices and may introduce additional retention limits. We will update this notice when those practices change.

8. Your requests

You can ask to access, correct or delete personal information you have sent us through the website by emailing jd@superimmersive8.com. We review each request individually and may ask you to confirm that you control the email address involved. Some records may need to be kept, for example records of our correspondence. If we keep any information, we will tell you what we kept and why.

Depending on where you live, you may have other rights over your personal information. To ask about them, email jd@superimmersive8.com.

9. Changes to this notice

We may update this notice. Each version has its own version identifier and effective date.
