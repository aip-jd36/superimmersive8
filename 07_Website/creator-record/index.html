Optional documents & automated readings

A reading reports what a document appears to show.

When you ask, an AI model reads a document you add and reports what it appears to show. No person at SI8 checks the reading, and it is kept visibly apart from what you told us. A reading is not verification of your declarations, does not establish that a document is genuine, and does not change which guidance you see.

What it establishes

  • An organised, fingerprinted account of what you declared
  • SI8's published educational guidance for what you named
  • A clear record of any documents' apparent contents

What it does not establish

  • Not legal advice and not a clearance
  • Not a certification or a human-reviewed assessment
  • Not verification of your answers or your documents
  • Not a determination of whether a tool or output may be used commercially
